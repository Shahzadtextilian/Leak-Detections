Early Warning Signs & 10-Minute Homeowner Meter Check

Hidden water and fuel leaks cause structural damage long before puddles appear across floorboards. Look for these early indicators around your City Heights home and perform a quick curb-meter verification before calling for acoustic pinpointing:

  • Spinning Curb Water Meter: Shut off every indoor faucet, ice maker, irrigation zone, and washing machine. Check the low-flow triangle or digital rate on your City of San Diego meter box for ten minutes; continuous movement proves an active hidden leak.
  • Warm Tiles or Hardwood Flooring: Localized heat patches beneath laminate, vinyl plank, or ceramic tile commonly signal an active hot-water copper pinhole leak below your concrete slab foundation.
  • Musty Baseboards & Bubbling Paint: Persistent dampness inside closets, kitchen toe-kicks, or lower hallway drywall often stems from weeping galvanized supply risers or aging cast-iron drain traps.
  • Sulfur Odor or Hissing Gas Lines: Dead grass above buried yard piping, whistling furnace connectors, or a rotten-egg mercaptan odor demand immediate evacuation and utility shutoff at the curb before repairs.
